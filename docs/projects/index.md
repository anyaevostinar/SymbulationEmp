# Projects using Symbulation

This is a running list of the projects that have used Symbulation!
If you would like to have your project added to this list, please
include a short description of you work, and a link to any git repository
that you may have. You can complete a pull request to add your
information to this list.  See [Getting Started with Symbulation Development](../dev/getting-started.md) for
information on how to complete a pull
request.

* Mutualistic symbionts' impact on host diversity and evolved complexity
  * Paper: [Endosymbiotic mutualism can constrain host diversity and evolved complexity](https://direct.mit.edu/isal/proceedings-abstract/isal2026/38/138224)
  * Symbulation v0.9

* Impacts of parasititic and mutualistic symbionts on evolved host complexity during intermittent stress events
  * Paper: [Saved by the Symbiont: Environmental Stress Intensity and Endosymbiont-Mediated Stress Response Determine Evolved Host Complexity](https://direct.mit.edu/isal/proceedings/isal2026/38/40/138185)
  * Symbulation v0.9

* Impacts of partner choice mechanisms on the evolution of mutualism versus parasitism
  * Paper: [Keep Your Friends Close and Your Enemies Distant: Partner Choice Can Enable Parasite Exclusion in Endosymbiotic Coevolution](https://direct.mit.edu/isal/proceedings-abstract/isal2025/37/134088)
  * Symbulation v0.9

* Evolution of de novo mutualism through large effect size mutations
  * Paper: [On the de novo Evolution of Mutualism in Symbulation](https://direct.mit.edu/isal/proceedings-abstract/isal2024/36/61/123524)
  * Symbulation v0.9

* Evolution of endosymbiosis through ectosymbiosis using complex (Avida-like) genomes
  * Paper: [Evolution of symbiotic task-based digital genomes: ectosymbiosis hastens the evolution of endosymbiosis](https://direct.mit.edu/isal/proceedings-abstract/isal2023/35/57/116926)
  * Symbulation v0.9

* Impact of an ectosymbiosis stage on the de novo evolution of obligate endosymbiosis
  * Paper: [Endosymbiosis or bust: Influence of ectosymbiosis on evolution of obligate endosymbiosis](https://direct.mit.edu/isal/proceedings-abstract/isal/34/112246)
  * Symbulation: v0.2
  * Git: [https://github.com/anyaevostinar/Evolution-of-Endosymbiosis-Paper](https://github.com/anyaevostinar/Evolution-of-Endosymbiosis-Paper)

* Evolution of lysis in bacteriophage
  * Paper: [Keep your frenemies closer: bacteriophage that benefit their hosts evolve to be more temperate](https://direct.mit.edu/isal/proceedings-abstract/isal/34/11/112253)
  * Symbulation: v0.2
  * Git: [https://github.com/anyaevostinar/Evolution-of-Lysogeny-Paper](https://github.com/anyaevostinar/Evolution-of-Lysogeny-Paper)

* Whether increased mutations during horizontal transmission selects for mutualism
  * Paper: [Dirty Transmission Hypothesis: Increased Mutations During Horizontal Transmission Can Select for Increased Levels of Mutualism in Endosymbionts](https://direct.mit.edu/isal/proceedings-abstract/isal/34/112312)
  * Symbulation: v0.2
  * Git: [https://github.com/anyaevostinar/Dirty-Transmission-Hypothesis-Paper](https://github.com/anyaevostinar/Dirty-Transmission-Hypothesis-Paper)

* Impacts of within-host ecology on host-symbiont mutualism vs. parasitism
  * Paper: [Evolutionary stability of host-endosymbiont mutualism is reduced by multi-infection](https://direct.mit.edu/isal/proceedings-abstract/isal/34/112319)
  * Symbulation: v0.1
  * Git: [https://github.com/DolsonLab/2021-Symbiont-Ecology](https://github.com/DolsonLab/2021-Symbiont-Ecology)
